YAMNet model and label mapping

The model is unchanged from android-vad's yamnet 2.0.10 artifact and Echo Speak 1.1.
Upstream: https://github.com/gkonovalov/android-vad/tree/2.0.10/yamnet/src/main/assets
yamnet.tflite SHA-256:
10c95ea3eb9a7bb4cb8bddf6feb023250381008177ac162ce169694d05c317de
yamnet_labels.txt is the embedded yamnet_label_list.txt extracted without changes.

The YAMNet model is from the TensorFlow Authors' YAMNet work:
https://github.com/tensorflow/models/tree/master/research/audioset/yamnet

and chronological, zero-padded window behavior (Apache License 2.0).
